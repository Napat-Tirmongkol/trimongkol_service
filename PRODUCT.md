# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Thai SME owners (restaurants, clinics, salons, shops) looking for a reliable software partner to build custom business applications (such as queue booking, POS, CRM, HR, inventory, or custom business websites).

## Product Purpose
Laravel-based public website for Tirmongkol Service — a software development studio delivering custom business systems for Thai SMEs, establishing storefront presence, credibility, and providing client onboarding.

## Brand Personality
Playful, friendly, and vibrant. Tone is approachable, clear, and reassuring, avoiding high-tech jargon to connect with non-technical business owners.

## Anti-references
- SaaS-cream / sand / beige color palettes (e.g., `--paper`, `--sand`, `--cream` backgrounds).
- Gradient text (`background-clip: text` combined with gradients).
- Side-stripe borders (borders > 1px as accents on cards/alerts).
- Excessive glassmorphic effects (blurs/glass cards used decoratively).
- AI slop grammar: tiny uppercase wide-tracked eyebrows or sequence numbers on every single section.

## Design Principles
1. **Friendly & Approachable Layout**: Leverage clean, structured card layouts (`rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl` with soft shadows) combined with playful elements.
2. **Clear & Direct Thai Translation**: Ensure translation matches natural Thai language without confusing IT jargon (e.g., "Leads" -> "ข้อความติดต่อ").
3. **Vibrant & Accessible Contrast**: Maintain high visual contrast (>= 4.5:1) for all body copy and active/disabled states. Never wash out text on light or dark backgrounds.
4. **Purposeful Imagery**: Use real, human-focused office or shop scene photography overlaid with gradient overlays to ensure absolute legibility of hero text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Standard WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios for body and interactive elements.
- Accessible and highly legible button states (especially disabled states).
- Motion restraint: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
